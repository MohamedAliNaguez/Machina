# TECHNICSAN product catalogue

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Professional buyers in Turkey and export markets looking for Cement bulker, LPG catalog, Compressors, Mixers or Silos. Visitors browse product types, describe their application and contact sales. Company staff maintain bilingual content; the owner of this project maintains the technical implementation.

## Product Purpose

A Turkish and English portfolio and catalogue for Ankara-based TECHNICSAN. Help visitors explore exactly five product types and submit relevant sales enquiries. There is no checkout or customer account system in the first release.

## Operating Context

The five fixed types, in order, are Cement bulker, LPG catalog, Compressors, Mixers and Silos. Pressure is the central brand idea. Do not add construction machinery categories or revive the old new/used browsing routes. Product pages support genuine photographs, specifications and brochures; until supplied they display honest placeholders and buyer requirements, without invented figures or stock claims. Routes use `/en/products/` and `/tr/urunler/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Astro and TypeScript render public pages; Sanity manages bilingual content; Cloudflare Pages/D1 and Resend support private enquiries. Keep development within free tiers and do not create paid services automatically. Maintain existing catalogue filters, articles, accessible forms, language equivalents, metadata, and deployment gates when changing the homepage.

The owner supplied the TECHNICSAN logo and “OUR PRESSURE. YOUR TREASURE.” signature. Exact company/legal details, contacts, actual products and their photographs remain to be verified. Do not invent business claims, measurements, authorised dealership relationships, prices, certifications or customer endorsements. Some mixers and silos may have different operating principles; the pressure-led identity does not establish a pressure rating for any product.

## Brand Commitments

The user selected the second homepage proposal, Machine Theatre, on 2026-10-20. No decorative arrows or terminal full stops in titles. Specific visual implementation belongs in DESIGN.md.

The selected steel reveal now opens onto TECHNICSAN's pressure artwork and wordmark. The user explicitly requested a 3–4-second introduction on the first visit, implemented as 3.5 seconds once per session. Preserve immediate dismissal, reduced-motion skipping and fail-safe access when scripts, media or storage fail.

## Product Principles

- Make equipment browsing and contact direct and readable
- Preserve equivalent Turkish and English journeys
- Distinguish examples from verified sales information
- Keep content crawlable and measure search visibility without promising rankings

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is the release target. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable mobile layouts, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and native form controls.
