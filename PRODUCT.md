# Machinery catalogue

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Professional construction machinery buyers in Turkey and export markets. Visitors browse equipment, assess suitability, and contact sales. Company staff maintain bilingual content; the owner of this project maintains the technical implementation.

## Product Purpose

A Turkish and English portfolio and catalogue for an Ankara-based machinery reseller. Help visitors find machines and submit relevant sales enquiries. There is no checkout or customer account system in the first release.

## Operating Context

New model pages describe configurations and applications. Used equipment pages describe individual units, including year, hours, condition, and available/reserved/sold status. Categories are the first major browsing section. New and used equipment receive equal prominence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Astro and TypeScript render public pages; Sanity manages bilingual content; Cloudflare Pages/D1 and Resend support private enquiries. Keep development within free tiers and do not create paid services automatically. Maintain existing catalogue filters, articles, accessible forms, language equivalents, metadata, and deployment gates when changing the homepage.

Company name, exact address, brands, and actual inventory are undecided. MACHINA is a replaceable preview identity. Do not invent business claims, measurements, authorised dealership relationships, prices, or customer endorsements.

## Brand Commitments

The user selected the second homepage proposal, Machine Theatre, on 2026-10-20. No decorative arrows or terminal full stops in titles. Specific visual implementation belongs in DESIGN.md.

The user also selected the second excavator loading proposal, The steel reveal. After finding the load-only version too easy to miss, the user explicitly approved a visible one-second introduction once per session. Preserve immediate dismissal, reduced-motion skipping and fail-safe access when scripts, media or storage fail.

## Evidence on Hand

Company details and actual equipment photographs remain unverified; illustrative artwork must be labelled accordingly.

## Product Principles

- Make equipment browsing and contact direct and readable
- Preserve equivalent Turkish and English journeys
- Distinguish examples from verified sales information
- Keep content crawlable and measure search visibility without promising rankings

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is the release target. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable mobile layouts, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and native form controls.
