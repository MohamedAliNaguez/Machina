States & motion

These are static visual mockups. They show layout and art direction; the controls inside the images do not perform website actions. The current website has not been changed.

Motion direction
  • Steel title bands: one short photographic entrance, with text and controls available immediately.
  • Image hover: a restrained 1.015 scale over 220 ms, without shifting the layout.
  • Gallery: transition only after selecting a photograph. No autoplay or infinite loops.
  • Filters and feedback: subtle transitions, clear focus and readable status messages.
  • Reduced-motion mode removes transforms, parallax and entrances.
Availability, empty results & enquiries
  • Reserved and sold states use explicit words as well as color. Sold pages keep useful information and offer alternatives.
  • Empty results preserve the query and offer Reset filters and Contact sales.
  • Errors preserve form input and explain what to fix. A receipt appears only after the enquiry is safely stored.
  • Pending contact and brochure rows are preview annotations; absent destinations are hidden from the finished public site.
Translations, brands & shared layouts
  • Turkish and English share the same templates. The Turkish mobile form demonstrates accented characters and longer labels.
  • Other categories and new-only or used-only views reuse the catalogue layout.
  • Brand equipment pages reuse the scoped catalogue. Brands appear in navigation only when verified content exists.
  • Sanity Studio remains the standard content editor, outside this public-interface mockup set.
